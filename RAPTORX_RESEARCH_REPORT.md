## 2. Revenue Architecture & Cash Flow Distribution
Unlike typical governance tokens with zero protocol claim, Jupiter produces substantial cash flow:
1. **Perp Trading Fees:** 75% of perpetual trading fees flow directly to JLP pool holders, maintaining an organic 40-75% APR.
2. **Launchpad Fees:** Protocol fees from LFG launchpad distributions are split between protocol development reserves and DAO treasury.
3. **Limit Order & DCA Fees:** 0.1% fees accrued on automated execution products.
4. **ASR Program:** Over 50M JUP + ecosystem tokens distributed quarterly to active governance participants who stake and vote on DAO proposals.
